# Source review

This is an internal account of how two pre-existing academic works informed a separate computational project. Neither work supplies transcriptomic measurements. Both originals are preserved unchanged in `private_sources/`, which is Git-ignored. The public analysis derives its results exclusively from GSE45827.

| Original | Used as background | Excluded |
|---|---|---|
| Case study essay (May 2026) | Breast-cancer sections on PR signalling, ER-targeted drugs, RANKL, WNT4, PI3K/AKT, MAPK, cell cycle, FOXO1, resistance. Candidate concepts were checked against the literature. | Assignment administration and declaration; illustrative patient narrative; all community-acquired MRSA sections and their references; original images. |
| Group breast-cancer risk-factor presentation | Broad risk-factor topics: age, family history, reproductive and hormonal exposures, adiposity, physical activity, smoking and alcohol. | Case-specific causal claims, third-party figures, slide graphics, private contributor and patient details. |

The presentation is collaborative work. Its five named contributors and slide credits are recorded in `private_sources/inventory.md`; this repository makes no claim that the presentation was solely authored by Tharuniya Anpalakan. The current computational analysis, code and original diagrams are new project work. Some source assertions were too categorical, including pathway effects treated as universal and case-level causal attribution, and were not carried into the new report.

The essay's fictional or educational case is not used as an observation. No source figure was reused. This project used AI assistance in drafting and programming; the author should review all claims and code before publication.

References were screened for relevance; MRSA citations were removed. The presentation attributes a Travis and Key breast-risk article to 2023 while giving the much older DOI `10.1186/bcr628`; that inconsistent entry was not carried forward. The essay's broad clinical and mechanistic claims were replaced by cited primary work or carefully bounded summaries. The new bibliography is selective rather than an unverified copy of either source list.
